01 A LITTLE ABOUT ME

I took the scenic route to navigation.

I began with software engineering in Xi’an, moved to Hong Kong for computer science, and eventually found a home in positioning and navigation. It turns out that code, learning, geometry, and satellites make a wonderfully strange team.

Today I’m a PhD student in the Intelligent Positioning and Navigation Laboratory at PolyU. I’m interested in systems that do more than produce a good answer—they should also know when that answer deserves our trust.
